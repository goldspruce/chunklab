# Quote Snapping vs. Entailment (Simple One-Paragraph Explanation)

**Sanity Engine & ScholarQA Verification Methodology**  
*Document Version: 1.0.0 — November 1, 2026*

---

Think of an open-book science exam: **Quote Snapping** checks if the quote is real—it algorithmically scans the actual textbook pages on physical disk to verify that the author *genuinely wrote those exact words*, ensuring the AI did not simply hallucinate a plausible-sounding citation from memory. **Entailment** checks if the quote is relevant—it acts like a strict human grader reading the quote in isolation and asking, *"Does this quote actually prove what you claim it proves, or did you just copy-paste a real sentence from the book to bluff me?"* In short, **Snapping stops fake citations** (fabrication), while **Entailment stops irrelevant citations** (citation bluffing), and an AI system must pass both to produce trustworthy scientific truth.
