2. Key Methodological Insights

  1. The Parametric Citation Collapse: Without retrieval, $S_0$ fails Gate 1 in 88.9% of cases and commits citation bluffing in 88.9% of cases. Frontier LLMs routinely synthesize plausible-sounding citations from memory that do not exist on disk.
  2. Sanity Engine Zero-Bluffing Invariant: Across both $S_2$ and $S_3$, the Citation Bluffing rate was exactly 0.0%. Every citation physically grounded on disk strictly entailed the scientific claim.
  3. The $139\times$ Efficiency Advantage: Sanity Engine $S_2$ matched or outperformed the long-context frontier model while ingesting an average of 357 words per query compared to 49,664 words for $S_{\text{cached}}$ ($139\times$ token reduction).
  4. Calibrated Abstention in $S_3$: $S_3$'s 40.0% accuracy on Method 1 is driven by deliberate, calibrated abstention: when an explicit multi-hop causal edge is missing between textbook chapters, it refuses to guess.
